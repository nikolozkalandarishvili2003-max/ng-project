About the Company

Prestige Motors Rental — Redefining the Road Since 2009 Founded in Tbilisi, Georgia, Prestige Motors Rental has grown from a fleet of 12 vehicles to one of the Caucasus region's most trusted automotive rental services. With over 15 years of experience, we've served more than 240,000 satisfied customers across Georgia, Armenia, and Azerbaijan. Our headquarters spans a 4,200 m² state-of-the-art facility, housing 380+ premium vehicles ranging from economy sedans to luxury SUVs. We believe driving isn't just transportation — it's an experience. Our story began when founder David Kvaratskhelia recognized a gap in the Georgian market for truly premium, reliable vehicle rentals. Starting with a handful of BMWs and Mercedes sedans, he built a company on the principles of trust, customer care. Today, Prestige Motors employs over 320 staff members, including certified automotive technicians, multilingual customer service agents, and dedicated fleet managers. We operate 8 Georgia — in Tbilisi, Batumi, Kutaisi, Borjomi, Signagi, Gudauri, Mestia, and Poti — ensuring our customers have access to premium mobility no matter where their journey takes them.

Our Fleet

380+ Vehicles. Every Journey Covered. Our meticulously maintained fleet includes vehicles from BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Audi, Toyota, and Range Rover. Every car undergoes a rigorous 47-point inspection before and after each rental. In 2023 alone, we refreshed 35% of our fleet with brand-new models, ensuring you always drive the latest technology. Whether you need a compact city car, a family-sized SUV, or a business-class executive sedan, our fleet advisors match you with the perfect vehicle in under 10 minutes. Our fleet is divided into six curated categories: Economy, Comfort, Business, SUV & Offroad, Premium Luxury, and Electric & Hybrid. The Economy range offers fuel-efficient options ideal for city driving, while our Luxury category features hand-selected vehicles such as the BMW 7 Series, Mercedes-Benz S-Class, Audi A8, and Range Rover Autobiography — each equipped with full leather interiors, advanced driver-assist systems, and premium sound. Our SUV & Offroad category is specially tailored for Georgia's diverse terrain, from Tbilisi's urban streets to the rugged mountain roads of Kazbegi and Svaneti.

Sustainability & Future

Driving Tomorrow, Today. At Prestige Motors, we're committed to a greener future. By 2026, 40% of our fleet will be fully electric or hybrid. We've already reduced our carbon footprint by 28% since 2020 through smart routing technology and solar-powered charging stations at our Tbilisi and Batumi locations. Our partnership with GreenDrive Georgia has resulted in 1,200 trees planted for every 10,000 rentals completed. The road ahead is electric — and we're leading the way. Our sustainability roadmap goes beyond vehicles. Our Tbilisi headquarters was redesigned in 2022 as a LEED-certified green building, consuming 41% less energy than a conventional facility of its size. We've eliminated all single-use plastics from our operations and introduced fully digital rental agreements, saving an estimated 1.8 million sheets of paper annually. In partnership with the Technical University of Georgia, we are co-developing an AI-powered fleet optimization system that will reduce idle vehicle time by 33% and cut unnecessary fuel consumption across our network.
